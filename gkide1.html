Part 5

Read the following passage. Decide whether statements from 23 to 26 are True or False and choose the correct answer.


Life in the countryside is different from life in the city. The countryside is usually quieter and more peaceful. There are fewer cars and fewer crowded streets, so people can enjoy the fresh air and beautiful natural scenery. Many people in rural areas work on farms. They often grow vegetables, raise animals, and work in the fields.

People in the countryside also have a strong sense of community. Neighbours often know each other well and are willing to help one another. At weekends, some people spend their free time doing outdoor activities such as riding bicycles, going fishing, or going for a walk. Teenagers may also hang out with their friends or take part in community activities.

However, life in the countryside is not always easy. There are fewer shops, entertainment centres, and public transport services than in cities. Young people sometimes move to cities because they want to find better educational or job opportunities. Despite these disadvantages, many people still enjoy countryside life because it is peaceful, friendly, and close to nature.
